Speed Lab

Set precise YouTube playback speeds without digging through menus.

Get supportRead privacy policy
Fast controls · remembered preferences · local settings

What it does

  • Set exact custom playback speeds.
  • Use presets for common speed changes.
  • Increase, decrease, reset, or restore the previous speed with shortcuts.
  • Hold a key for temporary 2× playback.
  • Remember speed in the current tab or across videos.
  • Use separate defaults for standard videos and Shorts.
  • Restore the preferred speed during YouTube navigation.
  • Handle browser speed limits without repeated technical errors.

Private by design

Speed Lab runs only on YouTube. It does not require an account, use analytics, collect video titles, or transmit browsing history.

Your settings remain in Chrome storage on your device.

Independent product

Speed Lab is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
